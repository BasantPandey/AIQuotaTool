Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no ads and no account. The extension has no server, so it costs nothing to run.

Does it read my chats?

No. It reads only the usage numbers that each provider shows on its own usage page.

Where is my data?

On your device, in local extension storage. Nothing goes to a server of ours. See the privacy policy.

Why does Copilot show no percent?

GitHub does not share a remaining percent for Copilot. The panel shows your plan status instead of a fake number.

A provider shows "Not connected". What do I do?

Open the provider website in the same Chrome profile and sign in. The panel updates within a minute.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub.
